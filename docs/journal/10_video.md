# Phase 10: Narrated tutorial video

## What we did
- `video/script.md`: 35 scenes. Each scene has a title, up to 5 bullets, one visual (a chart, a code image with
  line numbers, or a real log rendered like a terminal) and the narration.
- Numbers in the script are placeholders such as `{{routing.json:test_sub.pooled.router_acc|pct}}`, filled from
  `results/` at build time. The video therefore cannot contain a number the code did not produce.
- `video/build_video.py`: Pillow slides at 1920x1080 (DejaVu fonts; Noto for Hindi and Kannada; text wraps and
  shrinks to fit), Pygments code images, Piper narration (`en_US-lessac-medium`, length_scale 1.08) with 0.7 s of
  silence after each scene, one ffmpeg clip per scene (H.264 + AAC, yuv420p, 30 fps), joined with the concat
  demuxer. It also writes `video/tutorial.srt` and `video/chapters.md`. Unchanged scenes are reused (cached by a
  hash of the slide and the audio), so a rebuild after a small fix takes seconds.
- Pronunciation: the narration is written normally, and a word list in `build_video.py` (`PRONOUNCE`) swaps words
  for speech, e.g. PyTorch → "Pie Torch", NGINX → "engine X", LLM → "L L M". The subtitles keep the normal spelling.
- `video/check_video.py` (`make video-check`): duration 10-20 min, one video + one audio stream, 1920x1080 yuv420p,
  mean volume above -35 dB, subtitles and chapters exist, and one frame from the middle of every scene for review.
- The middle frame of every scene was checked for readable text, correct code and log lines, and colours.

## Result
- `make video` (`logs/10_video.log`): 35 scenes, 2,800 spoken words (after pronunciation expansion), 16.3 minutes.
- `make video-check` (`logs/10_video_check.log`): duration 16.26 min; h264 1920x1080 yuv420p 30 fps + audio;
  mean volume -19.8 dB; 35 frames extracted; `video-check: OK`.

## Errors and fixes
- The first script had 37 scenes (I wanted 25-35): merged the "plan" and "CPU speed" scenes into others.
- The load-test duration placeholder would have been spoken as "two m"; the slide now says "2 minutes".
- The terminal images used red / yellow / green window dots; the yellow is too close to amber, so the dots are grey.
- Frame review, round 1 (all 35 middle frames): 25 were fine. Scenes 4, 9, 11, 12, 15, 16, 18, 26, 27 and 29 had
  problems: log or code ranges that showed the wrong lines, code too small to read (27-39 lines), Latin text on
  Hindi/Kannada lines drawn as empty boxes, and the run-1 training chart still in orange. Fixes: ranges of at
  most 20 lines, a wider line-number gutter, one font per run of characters (DejaVu Mono for Latin, Noto for
  Devanagari and Kannada), the run-1 chart redrawn in purple, and the string-escape colour in code images
  changed from brownish orange to purple.
- Round 2: 7 of the 10 were fixed. Scenes 4, 11 and 18 were broken by two bugs in the builder: progress bars
  write a bare `\r`, which Python's `splitlines()` counts as a line break, so log ranges were off by 4 lines;
  and the new word-wrap dropped indentation. Now lines are split on `\n` only (like `sed`), the font shrinks
  before anything wraps, and indentation is kept. `tests/test_video.py` has a regression test, and the three
  scenes were checked again after the rebuild.

## How to explain this in an interview
1. The video is generated by code from the same results files as the README, so it can be rebuilt after any change.
2. Slides are drawn with Pillow, narration is offline text-to-speech, and ffmpeg joins one clip per scene.
3. An automatic check verifies length, audio level and resolution, and every scene's frame was reviewed.
